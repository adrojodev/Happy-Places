Happy Places 3.0 — your places, everywhere ✨

• Widgets! A live map of your happy places around you, right on your home screen (three sizes).
• Lock screen widget: save where you are with one tap.
• Control Center buttons (iOS 18): save this place or snap a photo without opening the app.
• "Snap a Place" widget: jumps straight into the camera — your photo's location becomes the new place.
• Siri: just say "Save this place in Happy Places".
• Lots of polish: smarter photo scanning, duplicate detection when saving nearby spots, and a smoother add-place flow.
