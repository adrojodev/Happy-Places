Notes for App Review — Happy Places 3.0.0

The app lets users save meaningful places on a map with an icon, color,
story, and photos. Data is stored on device (SwiftData) and synced through
the user's own iCloud account (CloudKit). No account or login is needed.

New in 3.0:
- WidgetKit extension: home screen map widget, lock screen quick-add,
  a camera quick-capture widget, and two Control Center controls (iOS 18+).
- App Intents / Siri App Shortcuts: "Save this place in Happy Places".

Permissions used:
- Location (when in use): to center the map and save the current spot.
  The widgets read only a cached location.
- Camera / photo library: to attach photos to saved places, and to suggest
  places from GPS-tagged photos (on-device scan; nothing is uploaded).

To test the widgets: long-press the home screen -> add widget -> search
"Happy Places". The lock screen widget is under Customize Lock Screen.
Control Center buttons appear in the Control Center gallery on iOS 18+.
